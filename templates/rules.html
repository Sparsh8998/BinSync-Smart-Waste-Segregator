Wishcycling FAQ

Are coffee cups recyclable? expand_more

Most paper coffee cups are lined with plastic or wax to prevent leaking. They belong in the garbage, not recycling or compost, unless explicitly marked as compostable by your municipality.

Do I need to wash containers? expand_more

Containers should be empty and lightly rinsed. Food residue can contaminate entire batches of recyclables, diverting them to landfills.

Can I bag my recyclables? expand_more

No. Keep recyclables loose in your bin. Plastic bags jam sorting machinery at recycling facilities.
