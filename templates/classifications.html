Pure Polymer

PET Soda & Water Bottles

#1 PET

Polyethylene Terephthalate rigid clear container. Highest market commodity value. Caps can remain loosely threaded.

Target Destination: Curbside Blue Bin
Preparation: Empty liquids & flatten
Pure Polymer

HDPE Milk Jugs & Bottles

#2 HDPE

High-Density Polyethylene translucent or opaque colored container. Easily remelted into drainage pipes and park benches.

Target Destination: Curbside Blue Bin
Preparation: Quick rinse, discard pump springs
Rigid Polymer

Polypropylene Food Containers

#5 PP

Yogurt cups, butter tubs, and takeout containers. Widely accepted in modern optical MRFs.

Target Destination: Curbside Blue Bin
Preparation: Scrape food remnants clean
Flexible Film

LDPE Film & Bubble Wrap

#4 LDPE

Clean grocery sacks, bubble wrap, and package cushioning. Banned from curbside bins because it tangles sorting rollers.

Target Destination: Storefront Soft Plastics
Crucial: Never put loose in blue bin
Composite Pouch

Laminated Snack Wrapper

BOPP/AL

Metallized polypropylene heat-bonded to aluminum vapor barrier. Cannot be pulped with standard plastic bottles.

Target Destination: Specialized Drop Depot
Contamination Risk: High (Jams MRF Belts)
Aseptic Carton

Tetra Pak Beverage Cartons

CARTON/AL

Multi-layer virgin paperboard layered with micro-thin polyethylene and aluminum. Hydrapulpable into tissue paper.

Target Destination: Curbside Blue Bin
Preparation: Empty, do not flatten
Pure Fiber

Clean Corrugated Cardboard

PAP 20

Clean shipping cartons and dry boxes. Recyclable up to 7 times before wood fibers become too short.

Target Destination: Curbside Blue Bin
Preparation: Flatten box, peel heavy plastic tape
Contaminated Fiber

Grease-Saturated Pizza Base

FIBER

Cardboard absorbed with grease and cheese remnants. Grease repels water during pulping and ruins entire batches.

Target Destination: Landfill / Organics (if local)
Pro Tip: Tear off clean lid for blue bin
Plastic-Lined Fiber

Disposable Hot Coffee Cup

PAPER/PE

Paper cup lined internally with a 5% polyethylene plastic coat. Banned from blue bins in most cities due to lining separation costs.

Target Destination: Landfill (Lid to Blue Bin)
Common Mistake: Top 3 Wishcycling Contaminant
Brittle Foam

Expanded Polystyrene (Styrofoam)

#6 PS

98% air volume, shatters easily in transit and contaminates paper streams with static foam beads. Banned from curbside bins.

Target Destination: Landfill / EPS Drop Depot
Alternative: Reuse packaging peanuts
Infinite Metal

Aluminum Beverage Cans & Foil

ALU 41

Infinitely recyclable metal. Uses 95% less energy to remelt than extracting bauxite ore. Clean foil can be balled together.

Target Destination: Curbside Blue Bin
Pro Tip: Ball foil into at least 2-inch sphere
Fire Hazard

Household Batteries (Li-ion & AA)

HAZMAT

Lithium-ion and alkaline batteries can ignite garbage trucks and sorting equipment if crushed. Never dispose in household bins.

Target Destination: Designated Battery Depot
Safety Step: Tape terminal ends with clear tape
